### Pillar 1: Story Bible
- **Logline**: A devout Soweto midwife delivers a baby during a township power blackout and notices a birthmark matching a legendary royal bloodline. When the wealthy Khumalo mining family arrives at dawn claiming the child, she discovers her own estranged daughter was the surrogate mother.
- **Genre**: High-Stakes African Melodrama / Vertical Microdrama (9:16)
- **Primary Language**: isiZulu
- **Target Episode Duration**: 90 seconds

### Pillar 2: Character Bible
1. **Thandiwe / Thandeka Sithole** (`PROTAGONIST`):
   - *Role*: 52-year-old devout Soweto community midwife.
   - *Core Motivation*: Protect the royal infant from corporate commodification and reconcile with her estranged daughter.
   - *Fatal Flaw*: Rigid moral righteousness that previously drove her family away.
   - *Signature Line*: *"A child is not platinum ore to be dug up and traded in Sandton, Bhekisisa."*
2. **Bhekisisa Khumalo** (`ANTAGONIST`):
   - *Role*: Autocratic Sandton mining tycoon.
   - *Core Motivation*: Secure an authentic male heir to retain ancestral mining leases before tribal reversion.
   - *Fatal Flaw*: Belief that sacred royal covenants can be settled with cash transfers.
   - *Signature Line*: *"That child carries the only bloodline that keeps my mining shafts open. Hand him over."*
3. **Lerato Sithole** (`SUPPORTING`):
   - *Role*: 24-year-old surrogate mother & Thandiwe's estranged daughter.
   - *Core Motivation*: Escape extortionate township loan sharks (Bra Mike) and protect her child.
   - *Signature Line*: *"Mama, forgive me... I had no other way to clear the loan sharks."*

### Pillar 3: World & Inviolable Invariants
- **Primary Settings**: Mofolo South township clinic (Soweto) juxtaposed with Sandhurst high-security executive compound (Johannesburg).
- **Rule 1**: Customary Zulu royal lineage covenants supersede commercial surrogacy contracts under customary law.
- **Rule 2**: Khumalo mining concessions remain valid only while direct royal bloodline is verified by the traditional council.

### Pillar 4: Chronology Spine
1. **Hook**: Midnight candlelight delivery during a rolling blackout; discovery of the royal ink-mark.
2. **Inciting Disruption**: Dawn arrival of Bhekisisa Khumalo's convoy; revelation of Lerato as surrogate.
3. **Point of No Return**: Thandiwe refuses Sandton settlement check and activates community protection.
4. **Midpoint Revelation**: Discovery of the 1912 Madadeni land covenant seal in the clinic emergency safe.
5. **Crisis**: Khumalo private security forces isolate the clinic, cutting emergency generator power.
6. **Climax Stand-off**: Thandiwe presents the infant and customary seal before the traditional council assembly.

### Pillar 5: Episode Architecture
- **Format**: 90-Second Vertical Microdrama Beats
  - *Beat 1 (00:00–00:15)*: Cold Open Hook — Delivery & Ink-Mark Discovery (Intensity 8/10).
  - *Beat 2 (00:15–00:50)*: Escalation Midpoint — Dawn Convoy & Surrogacy Confession (Intensity 9/10).
  - *Beat 3 (00:50–01:30)*: Cliffhanger Paywall Cut — Armed Enforcers vs Sjambok-wielding Township Community (Intensity 10/10).
- **Paywall Cliffhanger Prompt**: *"Will Thandiwe sign the Khumalo settlement or trigger a township uprising to protect the royal infant?"*

### Pillar 6: Narrative Plants & Payoffs
- **Plant 1**: Royal ink-mark / birthmark on infant's shoulder → *Payoff*: Unlocks tribal land rights over Khumalo shafts.
- **Plant 2**: 1912 Madadeni Land Covenant Seal in clinic cabinet → *Payoff*: Freezes corporate lease transfer.
